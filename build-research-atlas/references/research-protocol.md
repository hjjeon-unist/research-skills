# Evidence collection

## Scope and coverage

Record the seed question, search cutoff, inclusion criteria, queries actually run, and databases or sites consulted. A selective map is not a systematic review unless its search and screening methods support that label. Deduplicate preprint and proceedings versions; record the version used. Distinguish publication year from online/preprint date where it matters.

Begin with the original work and backward citations, then inspect forward citations and independent alternatives. Search deliberately for replication, failure cases, reanalysis, and criticism. Stop when the agreed scope is covered and new searches mostly repeat known work; disclose missing full texts and unexplored branches. Do not fill a quota with irrelevant papers.

## Paper evidence

Read the method, experiments, and limitations when accessible. For each significant claim record source IDs plus page, section, table, or figure locators. An abstract-only source supports only abstract-level claims. Do not report a benchmark improvement without its metric, comparison, and evaluation context.

Explain what enters the system, what leaves it, and how it is trained. Separate training and inference paths, target construction, loss, and recurrent/temporal state. Put technical detail after this intuitive explanation. For non-learning research, describe the experimental input, observation, and evaluation objective.

Team context has three distinct times: prior problem or program, the current contribution, and later direction. Use earlier/later papers or public institutional sources, not a speculative narrative about motives. Mark unavailable context plainly.

## Relationships and people

Paper edge types: antecedent, extension, application, replication, critique, alternative. Direction means source paper → related paper; critiques and alternatives can be later than their target and must be explained. A citation is evidence of citation, not necessarily conceptual dependence. Documented edges cite a direct statement or clear method comparison; inferred edges explicitly state the reasoning.

Researcher edge types: coauthor, supervision, collaboration. Coauthorship can be established by a shared paper; supervision requires an explicit biography, dissertation, or institutional statement. Keep personal relations distinct from conceptual relations between papers. Verify PI/group-leader/corresponding-author roles separately, record the institution at the study date, and avoid treating it as a current affiliation.

## Figures, portraits, and publication

Prefer an original mechanism figure that teaches the model over an ornamental thumbnail. Inspect the actual figure and keep its number, caption, source URL, credit, and applicable reuse terms. Use a verified portrait from an official profile if permitted; otherwise show an initials placeholder and the public source link. Do not infer a license from public availability.

Do not embed credentials, signed/private URLs, personal correspondence, participant-level data, unpublished notes, or copyrighted full-text PDFs in a public deliverable. The repository license covers its original code and guidance, not third-party media. Keep project assets separate and review the full embedded dataset before authorized publication.
